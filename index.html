About the club

A place to learn green technology by doing it

The Green Technology Club is a proposed student club for anyone who is curious about how sustainable technology actually works. You do not need an engineering background and you do not need to already know the topic. You need to be interested.

Members meet regularly to break down one technology at a time, from how a solar panel converts light into electricity to how a city uses sensor data to cut its energy use. Everything we publish on this site comes from those sessions.

What we focus on

Three areas, one goal

Green technology is a wide field, so we concentrate on three areas where students can realistically learn, build and make a difference.

Renewable Energy

Solar, wind, hydro and biomass — how each one generates power, what it costs, and where it works best in a tropical climate.

Explore renewable energy

Electric Vehicles

How EVs actually work, the different types on the road, charging infrastructure, and an honest look at their environmental impact.

Explore electric vehicles

Smart Cities

Transport, energy, buildings, waste and the sensor networks that connect them — including live air quality data read from a public API.

Explore smart cities

Why join

What you get out of it

Joining a club should give you something you cannot get from a lecture. Here is what we think that is.

Understand the field

Learn how green technologies work in practice, not just what they are called.

Build a network

Work with students from other courses who care about the same problems.

Practise real skills

Run a session, lead a project, present your findings to an audience.

Make a visible impact

Campus activities where you can measure what actually changed.
