My registrations

    Why the draft disappears

    Your half-finished form is held in sessionStorage, which the browser clears the moment this tab is closed.

    That is deliberate. The draft contains your name and email address, and on a shared computer — a library machine, for example — that should not still be sitting there for the next person.

    Why the saved list stays

    Once you submit, the registration moves into localStorage, which has no expiry. Close the browser, come back next week, and your list is still here.

    Nothing is sent to a server. This site is a student coursework project with no backend, so your details never leave your own device.
